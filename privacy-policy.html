Privacy Policy

Our commitment to protecting personal data in compliance with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 and international privacy principles.

1. Introduction

M3T Data Management Services (“M3T”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) is committed to safeguarding personal data in accordance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), its Implementing Rules and Regulations, and applicable international data protection standards.

2. Scope

  • Website visitors
  • Clients and prospective clients
  • Research respondents and participants
  • Partners and service providers

3. Personal Data Collected

  • Full name, email address, phone number
  • Company or organization
  • Service requested and project details
  • Optional file attachments
  • Technical and usage data (IP address, browser type)

4. Purpose of Processing

  • Responding to inquiries and service requests
  • Providing research and data management services
  • Client communication and project coordination
  • Legal and regulatory compliance
  • Website security and performance monitoring

5. Legal Basis

Personal data is processed based on consent, contractual necessity, legitimate interests, and legal obligations as permitted under RA 10173.

6. Data Security

  • Secure cloud-based storage
  • Encryption of data in transit and at rest
  • Role-based access controls
  • Audit logs and monitoring

7. Data Subject Rights

  • Right to be informed
  • Right to access and correct data
  • Right to object or request erasure
  • Right to data portability
  • Right to lodge a complaint with the National Privacy Commission
